Frequently asked questions

Where is my data stored?

Everything you enter stays on your device and syncs through your own private iCloud account. It isn't sent to us or to any central server, and it's never sold or used for ads.

How do I sync across devices?

Sign every device into the same iCloud account, and make sure Childhood Compass is switched on under iCloud in Settings. Your children, observations, terms and reports sync automatically. Give it a minute and make sure each device has an internet connection.

App preferences are stored per device rather than in iCloud, so a new device will run through first-time setup again even though your records are already there. Work through it and your existing children will be waiting on the other side.

My data isn't showing up on another device.

Check three things: the devices are signed into the same Apple ID, iCloud is turned on in the device Settings, and both devices are online. Sync can take a moment after adding new records.

How do I add or transfer students and centers?

Use the add buttons on the roster and setup screens. When adding a center, you can tap a suggested center to add it instantly, and tap it again to remove it if you change your mind.

How do I generate a report?

Open a student or a report view and choose the report you want. You can export it as a PDF and share it using the standard iOS share sheet.

How do I delete my data?

You can delete individual students, observations, or other records from within the app. To remove everything, delete the app and clear its data from your iCloud storage in device Settings.

Subscriptions & billing

How does the free trial work?

New subscribers get one month free. You choose a plan and confirm it with Apple at the start, but nothing is charged until the free month ends. Cancel any time before then and you pay nothing at all.

When does the free month start, and when would I be charged?

It starts the moment you subscribe, and the first payment is taken a month later unless you have cancelled. To see the exact date, open Settings on your device, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Childhood Compass — the renewal date is shown there. Apple also emails you before a free trial converts to a paid subscription.

Can I try it without subscribing first?

No — the free month runs through Apple, so you choose a plan up front. It costs nothing during that month, you are never asked for card details by us, and cancelling before the month is up means you are not billed.

Do I get another free month if I switch plans?

No. Apple allows one free trial per app, so moving between the monthly and yearly plans switches your plan without starting a second free month.

How do I cancel?

Open Settings on your device, tap your name at the top, then Subscriptions, and choose Childhood Compass. Cancelling stops future renewals; you keep access until the end of the period you have already paid for. Cancellation is handled entirely by Apple — we cannot start, stop or refund a subscription on your behalf.

I subscribed, but the app still asks me to subscribe.

Tap Restore Purchases on the subscription screen. That asks Apple to re-check your account, which fixes it in most cases — particularly on a new device, or after reinstalling. Make sure the device is signed into the same Apple Account you subscribed with, and that it has an internet connection.

What happens to my records if my subscription ends?

Nothing is deleted. Your children, observations and reports stay in your iCloud account and on your device exactly as you left them. The app does need an active subscription to open, so you would need to resubscribe to view or export them again.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple, not by us. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in, and choose the purchase. Do email us as well if something went wrong — we would like to know.
